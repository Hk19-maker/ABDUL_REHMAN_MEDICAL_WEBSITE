Chapter 7

Becoming a surgeon begins with medical education. Students first study foundational sciences: Anatomy Physiology Biochemistry Pathology Pharmacology These subjects build the scientific understanding necessary for clinical practice. During clinical years, students rotate through hospital departments. They observe surgeries, assist under supervision, and interact with patients. Professional communication and ethical responsibility are emphasized. Admission into medical school requires strong academic performance and success in entrance examinations. The journey is competitive and demanding.
