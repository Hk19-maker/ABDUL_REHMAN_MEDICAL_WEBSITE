Chapter 5

The 19th century transformed surgery completely. The introduction of anesthesia allowed patients to undergo operations without pain. Substances such as ether and chloroform made longer and more complex procedures possible. Shortly after, Joseph Lister introduced antiseptic techniques based on the understanding that microorganisms caused infections. Surgical instruments were sterilized. Surgeons washed their hands. Operating rooms became cleaner environments. These two developments — anesthesia and antisepsis — changed surgery from a desperate last attempt into a controlled and reliable medical specialty. Mortality rates dropped significantly. Surgical confidence increased worldwide.
