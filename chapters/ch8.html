Chapter 8

After completing medical school, graduates enter residency programs. Surgical residency is intensive and structured. Residents: Observe procedures Assist senior surgeons Gradually perform supervised operations Develop decision-making skills Responsibility increases with experience. Some surgeons pursue fellowships to specialize further in areas such as trauma, cardiac surgery, or neurosurgery. The training process requires dedication, long hours, and emotional resilience. However, it prepares surgeons to handle life-threatening situations with competence and confidence.
