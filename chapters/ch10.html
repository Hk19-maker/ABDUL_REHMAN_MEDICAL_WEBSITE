Chapter 10

Surgery continues to evolve rapidly. Modern innovations include: Minimally invasive surgery Laparoscopic procedures Robotic-assisted surgery 3D imaging and surgical planning Artificial intelligence support systems Researchers are developing artificial tissues, improved prosthetics, and advanced biomaterials. Technology enhances precision, but human judgment remains essential. The future of surgery will combine human expertise with technological innovation, always prioritizing patient safety and ethical responsibility.
