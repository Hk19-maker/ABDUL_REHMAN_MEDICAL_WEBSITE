Chapter 6

The 20th century witnessed rapid expansion in medical knowledge and technology. Surgery divided into specialized branches, including: General Surgery Neurosurgery Cardiac Surgery Orthopedic Surgery Pediatric Surgery Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery Vascular Surgery Each specialty requires focused training and deep expertise. Modern surgeons spend years mastering specific areas to provide safe and effective treatment. Advancements such as blood transfusion, antibiotics, improved anesthesia, and imaging technology further enhanced surgical safety and precision.
